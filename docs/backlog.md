# Local backlog

**Next free ID:** `OBS-94`

This board is canonical for OBS rows: engine execution, closed by a pull request and a release.
Product packages, priorities and operator gates are on the organization's board, cited here as
`parent: PB-nnn` in a row's Source (one parent at most; any other PB id there is a mention, and citing
a package does not close its operations). A row's status is changed only here, and the organization's
board derives a package's status from the rows that name it. Keep stable IDs, reserve each new one with agent-sync's `reserve OBS` before writing
its row, close with a receipt in Source, and retain closed rows. The workspace derives its common
backlog from [backlog-sources.json](backlog-sources.json). Dated handoffs remain historical evidence.

The section below is read as a format, not as Markdown: `tools/check_backlog.py` holds it to the
organization's register check. A Status cell is exactly one of `done`, `done in X.Y.Z`,
`done in X.Y.Z (#n)`, `done — merged (#n)`, `done — merged (#n), unreleased`, `cancelled` or
`superseded by OBS-n`, or `open`/`blocked` with an optional note. A closed status carries no note:
what was delivered is written in the Item column, after "Outcome:". A row holds no backslash and no
`<`, and the table is the last thing in its section.
